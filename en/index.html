Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes: free forever, no limits, no signup, no card. Signing on your device costs nothing per document to pass on.

Is the signature legally valid?

It produces verifiable PAdES signatures; with a qualified certificate it is the same class tools like AutoFirma make. Check what each procedure requires.

Is revocation checked?

The page queries the certificate OCSP status, and Adobe Reader verifies it when opening the PDF.

What are the limits?

PDFs up to 25 MB, RSA certificates, one signature per document. No document limit.

Does it work on Mac and Linux?

Yes: any modern browser, no Java, no installers.
